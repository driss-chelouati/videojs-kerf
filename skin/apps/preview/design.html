The numbers behind the cut

One radius system, two speeds, no exceptions.

Motion

Rise
200 ms, cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1). Revealed from its base, out of the band, never past the frame.
Sink
120 ms ease-in, back into the band. Faster out than in.
Poster
Before the first play the band folds away. A pointer, a tap or Tab brings it back at once.
Interrupted
Transitions, not keyframes: a menu closed mid-rise turns back from where it is.
Reduced
Every change is instant. Tabs still appear at their trigger.

Made for everyone

Accessibility is the structure, not a layer on top.

Readable over any picture

Controls never sit on footage. They sit on the frame: ink on frame is 17:1 on dark and 18:1 on light, the same over a white sky or a black night.

Big enough for a thumb

Calm on request

Reduced motion makes every change instant. Reduced transparency changes nothing: every surface is already solid.

Named for screen readers

Labels, states and announcements come from Video.js and its translations, including right to left.

Forced colours

Canvas for the frame, CanvasText for ink, Highlight for the fill and the focus ring.

Checked, not assumed

axe-core 4.10: 0 violations on both pages, dark and light, with the drawer and the settings menu open. Keyboard, focus, reduced motion and forced colours tested in Chromium.

Design thinking

Most players float their controls over the picture, then fight the picture for contrast with gradients and blur. Kerf cuts the picture instead. The controls live in a notch of the player's own frame, a solid surface in the host product's colour, so they read the same over a white sky or a black night.

Everything that opens rises out of that frame from the control that asked for it, and sinks back when the film plays. Transient feedback hangs from the top edge. One radius system, one accent, the official solid icons, tabular time. Familiar to use, unmistakable to look at.

Add Kerf to any page

Plain HTML and CSS on Video.js 10. Copy it in, own it.

  1. 1

    Install Video.js 10

    npm install @videojs/html

  2. 2

    Copy the skin

    Take src/video/ and src/styles/ from the Kerf repository, then import it once.

    import './video/skin';

  3. 3

    Wrap your video

    Put your media where the skin marks it. Any Video.js media works: a video file, HLS or Mux.

    <video-player>
  <!-- skin.html, with your media inside -->
  <mux-video src="https://stream.mux.com/{id}.m3u8"></mux-video>
</video-player>

  4. 4

    Set your look

    .kerf {
  --media-accent-color: #d4f53c;
  --media-border-radius: 20px;
}
/* on the skin root: data-surface="light" | "auto",
   data-motion="reduce", data-poster-controls="show" */
